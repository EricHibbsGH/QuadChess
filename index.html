About this game

Pass and play: each player takes their turn on this device. Or choose Play online in the toolbar — one player hosts and shares a room code, the other three join from their own computers, and moves travel directly between browsers over a peer-to-peer connection.

Your game is saved in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and online play never goes through a server of ours — only a public broker used to introduce the four browsers to each other.
